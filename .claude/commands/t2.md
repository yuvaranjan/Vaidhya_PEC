## 3. Rules you do not break

- **Stay in `apps/web/`**, and leave `app/api/specialist/route.ts` alone — that one is
  T1's. Never open `services/edge-ai/`; if the service misbehaves, check `/health` and
  tell T1 in your status file.
- **The theme is frozen.** Tokens and rules are in the comment at the top of
  `app/globals.css`. Light mode only, flat 2D, `shadow-sm` maximum, green for primary
  actions, amber and red only on urgency. No dark variant. No gradients.
- **Everything goes through `lib/edgeApi.ts`.** No component calls
  `fetch("http://localhost:8000")` directly.
- **The bot's English text is always on screen** beside the native-language audio, and
  the transcript shows both languages. Not optional.
- **Claim is a compare-and-swap** guarded on `status = 'awaiting_doctor'`. Zero rows back
  → 409 → toast and drop the row.
- Changing `packages/shared/*` means changing `services/edge-ai/contracts.py` in the same
  five minutes, with T1 present.
- You can build everything with `NEXT_PUBLIC_USE_MOCK_AI=true`. You are never blocked on T1.
